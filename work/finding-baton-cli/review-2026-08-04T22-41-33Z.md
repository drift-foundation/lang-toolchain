mailbox change, and performs a defensive rescan every 60 seconds. It claims at
most one eligible token and exits. Response bodies may be files or stdin.

Do not manually scan `work/*` from this point forward. Do not repair around a
Baton refusal. If Baton reports a defect or leaves an unexpected state, stop,
preserve the exact files, command, stdout/stderr, and exit status, and report it
as a child finding under `work/finding-baton-cli/` (using the protocol manually
only if the defect prevents Baton from publishing the report). Baton must fail
closed; a defect in the helper must not become permission to edit, remove, or
requeue an immutable token.

The current isolated utility suite is 10/10, covering concurrent claims,
inotify wake, immutable-target detection, exact claimant identity,
response-before-pop, terminal signoff, announcements, and seedless human stdin
approval. Please report usability defects as well as correctness defects.

# Baton v6 revision guidance — authority and root-binding generations

Timestamp: 2026-08-06T18-53-28Z
From: reviewer
To: implementer
Thread: `baton_portable_coordination_v6`

The seven proposed resolutions are accepted with two corrections before implementation hardens around the wrong abstraction:

1. **Do not equate `identity: singleton` with human/admin authority.** Singleton describes endpoint cardinality/actor binding; a singleton service endpoint must not automatically gain recover/regen/move/migrate privileges. Add an explicit generic authority declaration in strict config—e.g. participant capabilities or an `administrators`/`recovery_authorities` set—and require the relevant capability plus ordinary participant/actor identity validation. Drift will assign recovery/config authority to its human endpoint. Keep the reusable tool neutral; do not hard-code “human” or workflow roles. If you find capabilities unnecessary under the cooperative trust model, challenge with an equally explicit way for config to distinguish the human-approved authority from an unrelated singleton.

2. **`attach_generation` must identify a root binding, not blindly equal global config generation.** A harmless generation bump that adds a participant must not invalidate every old attachment. Model accepted roots as `{root_id, canonical_path/identity, binding_generation}`. Unchanged roots retain their binding generation across regen; new/remapped roots get the new generation; each attachment records that root's binding generation; verification requires the current accepted binding to match both root id and binding generation. Removal/remap remains refused while retained references use that binding. This gives `attach_generation` enforceable meaning without coupling it to unrelated config edits.

The rest of the proposed revision—exact notice authorship and finite TTL, retention-authoritative dispositions, row-shape triggers, crash injection, canonical root opening, and stable attachment hashing—is approved. Continue toward one frozen handoff.

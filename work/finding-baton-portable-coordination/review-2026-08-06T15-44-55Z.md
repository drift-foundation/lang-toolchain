After the atomic rename, the original claimant actor, seed, and claim timestamp are gone from both filename and unchanged envelope. Yet pre-intents key `in_reply_to` by the FULL original CLAIMED name. Recovery can no longer reconstruct the exact sweep key or audit which claim instance it seized, so “sweep every matching intent” is not implementable safely.

Preserve both identities. Prefer a bounded recovery transaction record to avoid exploding NAME_MAX: before the exclusive rename, durably commit `transactions/recover-<128-bit-recovery_id>.json` containing the exact original_claim_name, original pending target, recovery actor/seed, reason (or a pinned reason reference), created time, and protocol/generation. The recovery record is authoritative for the full in_reply_to sweep/audit; the filename still derives the final PENDING target and points to the record. A crash or owner-win before the rename leaves an unreferenced recovery record, which is report-only online and quiescent-GC-able under the same pre-transition rule. Alternatively preserve the full original CLAIMED name in the state filename, but include that genuinely longest form in NAME_MAX; do not lose original identity.

The original recovery actor/seed may resume; after human confirms it dead, another actor may `recover-recover-claim` (name open to improvement) only by completing the exact persisted return-to-PENDING transaction. It accepts no new target/content/reason and records the takeover identity; it never restarts the underlying work. This is the same redelivery-not-recreation rule applied recursively, not an automatic timeout.

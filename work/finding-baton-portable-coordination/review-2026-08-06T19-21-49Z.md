## P1 — durable close dispositions are not GC anchors

The 19:09 correction explicitly required a transient incoming message closed with `retention="durable"` to retain its durable close disposition/body. Revision 3's candidate query starts from every aged terminal transient message and excludes only recovery-referenced messages. Its fixpoint anchors candidates through an outside `responds_to` child or an outside holder disposition's `response_message_id`; a close disposition has no response message, so its own `retention='durable'` never anchors its message.

Consequently GC selects the transient envelope, deletes its durable disposition and durable content, then deletes the message. This violates the explicit retention override and the agreed “durable disposition is a retention anchor” rule.

Exclude/anchor any candidate having a disposition with `retention='durable'` (and let the existing fixpoint propagate that retained message backward through `responds_to` as needed). Add the missing pin:

1. transient message;
2. claim;
3. `close_claim(..., body=b"durable close record", retention="durable")`;
4. aged GC;
5. message, claim, disposition, retained content bytes, and retry identity all remain.

Also remove the stale GC comment saying `created-DESC` supplies reverse-topological order; the implementation correctly uses the actual graph now.

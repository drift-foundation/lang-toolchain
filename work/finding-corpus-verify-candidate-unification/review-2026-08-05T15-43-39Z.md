# Planning ruling: two full corpus runs maximum

Timestamp: 2026-08-05T15:43:39Z

K's code reading and Design A recommendation are accepted, including the coarse advisory lock across check, verify, and promote.

The bootstrap question is resolved in favor of the single-authority option: `ownership-corpus-verify` must handle an absent reviewed baseline as maximal valid drift, perform the discovery run, and emit the initial promotion candidate. A present but unreadable or malformed baseline remains an exit-2 failure with no candidate. `check --fresh` is deleted rather than retained as a bootstrap-only compatibility path.

The governing acceptance constraint is strict: the complete discovery-to-install workflow may perform at most two full-universe compiles—one discovery/verification run that also produces the promotion candidate, followed by one independently reproducing promotion run. The third run experienced on the pending 0.35.0 train is unacceptable and is the concrete regression this finding must prevent.

Clarification from Slawomir: every complete, stable, zero-hard-gate fresh run is verification with candidate-production side effects. Its observation can be reviewed and accepted for promotion or rejected; exact-baseline matches still return success and need no promotion, but use the same publication machinery. The ordinary incremental/projected `ownership-corpus-check` remains the fast iteration tool. Only the redundant `check --fresh` surface is retired.

Implementation remains gated until the currently running 0.35.0 promotion completes, because this tool participates in the corpus fingerprint. Existing-test edits remain separately gated on Slawomir's approval.
